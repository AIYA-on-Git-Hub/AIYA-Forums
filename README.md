# AIYA Programming Toolkit 🛠️

This repository provides technical resources, exercises, problem sets, and framework starter kits supporting the **AI Youth Alliance (AIYA)** programming.

AIYA programming gives students opportunities to explore artificial intelligence through hands-on experimentation, collaborative problem-solving, technical projects, and critical evaluation.

The resources in this repository support three areas of AIYA programming:

- **💻 Coding** — hands-on software development, machine learning, AI programming, and building AI agents.
- **📈 Finance** — exploration of artificial intelligence in economic modeling, financial analysis, predictive workflows, and related applications.
- **🗳️ Democracy & Media** — exploration of the impact of artificial intelligence on media integrity, misinformation, democratic discourse, and political outcomes.

For the current description of AIYA's programming and opportunities for participation, visit:

**[AIYA Programming](https://www.aiyouthalliance.org/programming)**

## 📁 Repository Structure

*UNDER CONSTRUCTION*

- `/finance-toolkit/` — Python notebooks, datasets, and exercises for exploring predictive analysis, economic modeling, financial analysis, and related applications of AI.

- `/democracy-&-the-media-toolkit/` — Frameworks and resources for examining the impact of AI on media integrity, misinformation, democratic discourse, and political outcomes, including exploration of synthetic media, text and voice patterns, and AI evaluation and classification techniques.

## ⚠️ Academic Notice & Cognitive Well-Being

AIYA encourages students to engage with artificial intelligence critically and deliberately.

AI should be used as a **force multiplier for the mind**, not as a replacement for independent thought, analysis, subject mastery, creativity, or genuine authorship.

The resources in this repository are intended to help students explore both the capabilities and limitations of AI through direct experience.

Students should:

- Question AI-generated outputs
- Verify information independently
- Understand the limitations of AI systems
- Maintain independent analytical capacity
- Develop genuine subject understanding
- Preserve agency and authorship in their work

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository to your local machine.
2. Review the toolkit corresponding to the area of AIYA programming you are exploring.
3. Follow the instructions and requirements provided with each resource.
4. Coordinate with your school's **Faculty or Administrative Advisor** where appropriate.
5. Explore the current AIYA programming at **[aiyouthalliance.org/programming](https://www.aiyouthalliance.org/programming)**.
6. For additional support, contact AIYA at **info@aiyouthalliance.org**.
